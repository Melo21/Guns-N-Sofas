# Credits

## Guitar samples

The acoustic guitar sound in `samples/` comes from the **guitar-acoustic** samples in
[nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), installed from the
`tonejs-instrument-guitar-acoustic-mp3` package.

- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- **Changes made for this app:** 11 notes were selected, each trimmed to 2.6 seconds with a 0.6 second
  fade-out, and converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 80 kbps. The app pitch-shifts each sample to reach
  nearby notes.
- The tonejs-instruments project describes its samples as coming from public domain sources, edited for
  consistency.

The CC BY 3.0 license requires this attribution to stay with the samples if you reuse them.

## Songs and chord charts

The chord charts are simplified practice arrangements written for this app. They are not official
transcriptions and may differ from the recordings in key, structure, or timing. No lyrics, tablature, or
audio recordings of the songs are included. Song titles and artist names belong to their respective
owners and are used only to identify the songs.
